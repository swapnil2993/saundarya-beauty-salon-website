About

It's All About My Story

Hello, I’m Vibhuti Pande, a professional beautician with over 11 years of experience in the beauty and wellness industry. My journey began in Sambhaji Nagar, where I completed a Professional Beautician Course and started providing a wide range of beauty services.

Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of working with numerous clients, enhancing my skills and understanding of personalized beauty care. I’ve also participated in several beauty events and training programs to stay updated with the latest trends and techniques.

To further refine my artistry, I attended a makeup crash course in Mumbai, where I had the opportunity to work as an assistant makeup artist for a Marathi film actor— an experience that broadened my creative and professional expertise.

For the past three years, I’ve been successfully running my own salon in Pune, dedicated to offering high-quality beauty and makeup services in a warm and welcoming environment. My goal is to help every client look and feel their best through skill, care, and a passion for beauty.
